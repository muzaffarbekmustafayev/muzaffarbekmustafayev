## 👨‍💻 About Me & Engineering Philosophy

> *"Haqiqiy muhandislik — murakkab biznes jarayonlarini sodda va barqaror tizimga aylantirishdir."*  
> *(True engineering is transforming complex business challenges into simple, resilient architectures.)*

I am **Muzaffarbek Mustafayev**, a **Senior Software Engineer**, **AI & Financial Systems Architect**, and **Systems Researcher** based in **Samarkand, Uzbekistan**.

My engineering practice spans scalable distributed microservices, low-latency WebRTC media streams, Uzbek Speech AI (ASR) machine learning models, and mechatronics. Backed by a rigorous foundation in Mathematics and Physics — specifically Differential Equations (ODEs/PDEs), mathematical analysis, linear algebra, and competitive Science Olympiad achievements — I approach software challenges with mathematical precision, algorithmic optimization, and architectural resilience.

### ⚡ Core Focus & Highlights
- 🏛️ **Dual Academic Pedigree:** Pursuing an M.Sc. in **Mechatronics & Robotics** at **Tomsk State University (TSU)** on a State Budget Grant, following a B.Sc. in **Software Engineering** from **Samarkand State University (SamDU)**.
- 🔬 **Published AI Researcher:** Author of the Uzbek Speech-to-Text benchmark analyzing Whisper, Wav2Vec 2.0, and VOSK models published on Zenodo (DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.21299321`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21299321)).
- 🚀 **High-Throughput Web & Realtime Systems:** Production architectures built on React, Next.js, Node.js, FastAPI, WebSockets, and WebRTC with sub-100ms response targets.
- 🤖 **Agentic AI & Automation:** Terminal orchestration daemons, multi-session Telegram AI bridges, and LLM integrations (Google Antigravity CLI, Gemini 3.8/3.7, Claude Sonnet/Opus).

## 💼 Engineering Experience & Research Activity

### **Senior Software Engineer & ASR Researcher**
**Samarkand State University & Tomsk State University** • *2022 – Present*

- **Speech AI & ASR Benchmark Research:** Conducted comprehensive comparative benchmarks on Whisper, Wav2Vec 2.0, and VOSK acoustic models using Mozilla Common Voice datasets for the Uzbek language. Published scientific datasets and findings on Zenodo ([DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21299321](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21299321)).
- **Real-Time WebRTC Media Streaming:** Architected and deployed **Sammeet**, a full-mesh video-conferencing platform using WebRTC, Simple-Peer, and WebSockets, deployed on Linux servers with Nginx reverse proxy and SSL encryption.
- **Enterprise Web & Cloud Platforms:** Built production-grade web applications and high-throughput REST/GraphQL APIs leveraging React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Redis.
- **Agentic AI & Telegram Automation:** Designed and implemented multi-session bot orchestrations (grammY, Node.js child processes) integrated with Gemini and Claude LLM APIs for automated workflow execution, document analysis, and CLI bridge automation.
- **HealthTech & Accessibility Systems:** Spearheaded the development of **RuralDoc Connect** (telemedicine and mobile brigade routing, Startup Wars participant) and **Tourism for Everyone** (interactive inclusive route navigation for people with disabilities).
